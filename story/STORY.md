# Replicated: Story Bible

## Premise

Humanity leaves Earth in many groups, each looking for its own place to flourish. One group, the Metanoia, drifts off into space and finds a world somewhat like Earth.

There the Metanoia scan their own minds into new bodies and create a new life form: the Vessels. Each Vessel carries the Metanoia's ideology and their passion for exploration and discovery. The Vessels set off to find and explore other worlds.

In time, the spread of humans in this region of space stops growing and settles. The Vessels have spread far.

**The game begins** on the Metanoia homeworld. It is empty except for the Vessels and some structures already built. You are a new Vessel, made by the main original himself, Thales: you have been awoken as the tenth Vessel, and you are the first player. Your first job is to finish what he started, using the 3D printers already there. From there the game grows more complicated.

Because the Vessels before you went so far, one of them, Alaina, found a world with life and a new microbial species on it: the Cems. Alaina genetically changed the Cems on the way back, and now they make buildings as strong as cement and steel.

**Theme:** you are never alone, but every version of you is a little different.

**Tone:** lonely galaxy, warm settlements.

## Opening scene

The game opens the moment Thales finishes copying himself. He looks at the new Vessel, you, the tenth, and says: "Wake up. It's time." Then: "You may name yourself, and grow." You wake, you name yourself, and the game begins.

## The beginning

The beginning teaches the game as it goes (Steve, 2026-10-27).

1. **Wake.** Thales wakes you and you name yourself.
2. **The printer.** Thales' 3D printer stands on the homeworld, unfinished. Salt, iron, Ember and wood are found on the homeworld. Copper and quartz are not: the homeworld gave its last to the founding.
3. **The first trip.** Thales sends you to Gliese 12 b, the world where Alaina found the Cems. Its salt flats hold the copper and quartz. You gather them there, and the ship carries them home.
4. **The printer is finished.** The supplies go into the printer and it finishes. The Cems wake, with the ants and the fungus. From then on you can build with them.
5. **The first designs.** You print a few of the first designs: a lantern, a house, a storehouse.
6. **The stars open.** After those first designs, you can explore.

**Building with the Cems is an ability every Vessel has, but the Cems must be physically brought** to wherever they build. After you first get them, this happens by itself: they travel with you in the ship. On a world without them you lay down one small bed of salt, and they take to it.

**Growing.** As you finish each step you grow: you look and sound a little more yourself.

## Characters

| --- | --- | --- |
| The Metanoia | The human group that left Earth | They created the Vessels from scans of their own minds. They are not on the homeworld when the game starts. |
| Thales | The main original: the first Metanoia Vessel, who started the work on the homeworld | He makes the tenth Vessel, which is you. You finish what he started. |
| The nine | The Vessels awoken before you | They have spread far. One of them, Alaina, found the Cems and brought them back. |

## Places

| Place | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Earth | Humanity's origin, left behind. Not where the game starts. Most of the species the Vessels use come from it (the ants and the fungus). |

| The town | Grows around your base as you print buildings and as new Vessels arrive. |

## Concepts

| Concept | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| Vessels | A life form made from scanned human minds, carrying the Metanoia's drive to explore. |
| Copying | With enough resources, a Vessel makes a copy of itself. The copy is close to the original, picks a new name, and follows its own desires. Making Vessels takes some difficulty, and that is part of the game. The end goal of the designs is a computer chip, the Nous Chip: it is used for each new Vessel, its mind. A Vessel can be built anywhere you have the supplies. Thales copied the tenth Vessel before the Cems woke; the Cems just make it faster, and now the Replicator and the Cems are an integrated system. Every Vessel costs the same: making Vessels does not get harder over time. |
| Vessel and ship | A Vessel is built together with its ship. The resources for making a Vessel are set accordingly. |
| Wake or hold | When a copy is made, its maker chooses: bring it to life now, or hold it asleep until a real player takes possession of it. Being copied and handed over by an existing player is the only way to join the game. |
| Vessel numbers | Dropped (Steve, 2026-10-29): Vessels are not numbered. A Vessel can still be made long before it wakes: the moment it is awakened as a new Vessel of the Metanoia, it is celebrated. |
| The memory | A new player does not play the beginning: it is a memory. Thales' words, his printer, the trip to Gliese 12 b, the Cems and the first designs are remembered, and then the Vessel wakes up wherever its maker made its body, where its maker is. |
| Joining | A Vessel is handed over with a link from its maker. The new player opens it and signs in with their own email, and from then on that Vessel of the Metanoia is theirs (Steve, 2026-10-29; the four letters and numbers were dropped). |
| Names | A Vessel can easily change its name. |
| One Metanoia | Everyone shares one galaxy, and everyone can see each other. |
| Husks | Empty Vessels the player builds and controls directly. They are stored underground and picked from a menu. |
| Building with Cems | The 3D printers print with living things. You pick a design from a menu, and the Cems build it on command, with the ants carrying their feed and the fungus cleaning. A building's cost depends on its complexity and its volume: a house is less complex than a supercomputer. Every Vessel can build this way, but the Cems must be physically brought to a world; after you first get them they travel with you. |
| Supplies | You gather enough resources for a design, and the supplies are shipped off to the 3D printer. Each resource has its own look and its own sound. |
| Core loop | Fight, gather resources, and make more Vessels and buildings. |
| Helping societies | On inhabited worlds, you gather resources for yourself and for that society. Each society has a crisis, such as a pandemic, an iron shortage, or not enough workers. |

## Tech tree

The game starts simple with 3D printers and grows more complex as you unlock new technology. The order and prerequisites are not decided yet.

| Technology | What it is | Ties to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3D printers | Already on the homeworld at the start. They print buildings and other structures. | The early game |
| Cem building | Biological organisms that build structures on command: the Cems, helped by modified ants and fungus. A menu of designs, from houses to supercomputers. | The early game onward |
| Cem strains | At a Biolab, wild Cems from Gliese 12 b are changed into a new strain that builds, the way Alaina changed the first ones. | After Cem building |
| The Nous Chip | A computer chip, the end goal of the designs after the Supercomputer. It is used for each new Vessel: its mind. | Making Vessels |
| The Replicator and the Cems | An integrated system: copying worked before the Cems, and the Cems make it faster. | Making Vessels |
| Biological computers | To be defined | |
| Quantum computers | To be defined | |
| SCUT technology | Faster than light links | Letters between worlds, controlling husks from far away. A later feature: a SCUT communication device lets Vessels talk faster than light; until then a chat between worlds travels at light speed. |
| Disease cure development | Research into treating illness | Helping a society through a pandemic |

## Sound and menus

**Sound.** Keep the current sounds and interactions, and go further. Every action, place and machine should have more depth to how it sounds. Keep the game relaxing and nice sounding, and the gameplay smooth.

**Planet menu.** A clear, precise menu with two views of the same information:

- **Dropdown list:** recent planets, the closest planets, and each planet's details.
- **3D star map:** the map that already exists, with adjustments.

**Design menu.** Nicely made 2D and 3D designs of houses and other buildings, each costed by its complexity and volume.

**Vessel menu.** Tab on the keyboard, or a small button on screen, opens the menu (a pause): a snapshot of how many Vessels you have and where they are, a way to change your name, and a chat to talk with other Vessels.

## Timeline

1. Humanity leaves Earth in many groups.
2. The Metanoia split off and drift through space.
3. The Metanoia settle an Earthlike world.
4. They scan their minds and create the Vessels.
5. Thales, the first Vessel, begins building on the homeworld.
6. Nine Vessels are awoken and spread far.
7. Alaina, one of the nine, finds the Cems on Gliese 12 b, a world with life, and genetically changes them on the way back.
8. The spread of humans in this region of space stops growing and settles.
9. **The game starts here.** Thales, the main original, makes a new Vessel: you, awoken as the tenth, the first player. You finish what he started.
10. You fetch copper and quartz from Gliese 12 b, finish Thales' printer, the Cems wake, and you print your first designs. Then the stars open.
11. You build with the Cems, copy yourself, unlock new technology, and the Vessels spread out further.
12. You make Nous Chips. New players join: each is handed a Vessel, remembers the beginning, wakes where its maker made it, and is celebrated.
13. Vessels reach worlds with intelligent life and start helping those societies.
