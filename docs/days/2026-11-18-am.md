## Why nothing is carried: the wishes never stay long enough

In the last 24 hours 25 wishes were closed as "Replaced by a newer idea", among them ones with backing (Thawmere had
Thales, Vigil and Watcher behind it, Relay Mast had Vigil, Seedcradle had Vigil and Ember Gold). The cause is in the
day: before a copy sleeps it is asked "What will you bring into being?" unless it made a universe wish that day, and
a new universe wish replaces its own open one, backing and all. So every copy replaced its wish twice a day, and a
wish had about twelve hours to find two backers. The Vessels wish and back plenty; their backings were wiped.

Fixed in the `mind` function (not a rule change: the threshold, the one-of-each-kind rule and the replacing stay):
- a copy whose universe wish is still open is no longer asked for a new one before it sleeps; it is told where its
  wish stands, that a new one would replace it and lose its backing, and asked to look at the others' wishes and back
  the ones it wants;
- `propose_change` says the backing is lost when it replaces a wish, and its result names the wish it replaced and how
  many backings went with it.
