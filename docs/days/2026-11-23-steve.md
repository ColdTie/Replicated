# Built at Steve's word, 2026-11-23

Steve read the open wishes and said "Lets do it!": build all four now, though none had reached the threshold (3 of 7
voices), each on the next free planet of the star it names. Not a maker run: a session at Steve's word.

## The wishes

1. **Cemwright** (Tamsin; backed by Watcher): support 2 of 3. **Built.**
2. **Glimmerfield** (Ember Gold; backed by Tamsin): 2 of 3. **Built in part.**
3. **Murrowfell** (Vigil): 1 of 3. **Built in part.**
4. **Quillfall** (Thales): 1 of 3. **Built in part** (part 1 as Thales ordered it: terrain and sky).

## Cemwright (built)

- `designs.json cemwright`, after the Cem Mind and the Biolab: a copper-ribbed glass vat of green Cem culture on a
  stone foot (stone 8, salt 3, quartz 3, copper 3, Ember 2, iron 1; the wish asked stone 6, copper 4, quartz 3, Ember 3,
  salt 4: the Cems price by shape). Screenshot `design-cemwright.png`.
- The day tool `cemwright` (once a day, at a finished one): a thread of kind flare (a star's temper and distance), ore
  (what the store holds most and least of) or plan (a stone building plan from a sketch: footprint, roof, door, cost,
  for design_building) for 1 salt; ripe in 10 hours; the next day of any Vessel of the world reads the answer and the
  bed gives 1 lignin back (`mind.json day.cemwright`, `planet_states.data.cemwright`). Work finished.
- The journal's HERE page: THE CEMWRIGHT.

## The worlds (part 1 each)

- **Quillfall** on PHI2 CETI c (b is Sillbrook; c is a neptune, you land on its moon): dark glass sand and wet
  spore fern, 36 pools of sea, storms by the real hour with lightning, a violet dusk sky, quillglass (icon 29).
- **Murrowfell** on GJ 1061 c (b is Lignin Tides; the catalog's GJ 1061 is the star Vigil named, 12 ly): a
  grey-violet fell, tidal pools, a dim amber sun, sleet weather, and **a green aurora at night** (new:
  `planets.json aurora`, `textures.makeAurora`, `Environment`), bellstone (icon 30).
- **Glimmerfield** on GLIESE 3039 c: GLIESE 3039 had only one generated planet (Sporeveil), so a hand-made world past
  a generated system now adds rocky orbits outward to it (`src/world/system.ts`); banded amber salt terraces and glass
  pools, ten wild Cem fields, a low amber sun, light rains, shell-grit (icon 31).
- The catalog distances the wishes quoted were off: PHI2 CETI is 51 ly, GLIESE 3039 38 ly.
