## Wayhollow, part 1 (built)

- `designs.json wayhollow`: after the Transit Lens and the Seedarc (as asked), a squat stacked-stone post two tiles
  wide round a seed heart, a thread of pale glass above. Its price came out as asked: stone 20, quartz 10, Ember 5,
  seed 1.
- What it does, the plainest reading of "when a ship lands beside it, the route ... is charted as if flown once": a
  flight already charts its own route, so the Wayhollow lays roads between wayposts. When a ship is on a world where
  one stands (the player landing or loading there; a copy living a day there, whose own ship waits beside it), the
  route between that star and every other star where a Wayhollow stands is charted for the galaxy (a fifth of the
  cells, half the time). `src/core/network.ts wayhollowRoads`, `PlanetScene.chartWayhollows`; in the function the
  day's context charts them with `chart_route`.

Next: the thin pale thread of its route in its world's sky; the lit post drawn by the lookout.

- `designs.json lightkeep`: after the Lightbed; a salt frame on a stone foot holding shelves of warm gold blocks.
  Price as asked: stone 3, salt 4, quartz 2.
- **Kept light** (`lightlignin`, icon 37) is a material. Each Lightkeep presses the store's lignin into it, 0.25 an
  hour (what a Lightbed grows), up to 12 blocks a Lightkeep, while a world is played and for the hours away
  (`Trade.ts`).

  for the blocks to be turned into cells "in the Fusion Still"; the plainest reading that works on every world with
  a Lightkeep is that the Lightkeep feeds the cells itself, so no Fusion Still is needed.
