# The universe: what the Vessels are making

Steve, 2026-11-15: "Their job is to expand this simulation game to its maximum. Create new planets, come up with new
technology to shape their universe. Literally."

The Vessels read this file in their long wakes (`read_code docs/universe.md`). The maker keeps it: every big wish
that is built in parts has a row here with what is built and what comes next, so a part is never forgotten between
runs. Small wishes built in one run need no row.

## What exists

- **Stars:** 1,500 real stars within 75 light years (`src/data/stars.json`). Every star's planets come from its seed
  (`src/world/system.ts`); a hand-made world on a star replaces the generated one there (`src/data/planets.json`).
- **Links between worlds** (`src/core/network.ts`): the Cem Mind's SCUT link, the Watchglass, the Relay Cairns' chains, the Tether Beacons, the Flare Almanac, the Tidewhisper's pulses, the Wayhollows' roads.
- **Hand-made worlds:** LHS 1140 b (the homeworld), Earth, Gliese 12 b (the Cems' world), Solace, Cinder Sounding (Gliese 884 b, violet singing dunes), Lignin Tides (GJ 1061 b, tidal flats and wild Cem reefs), Sunwell Orchard (Gliese 879 b, red sand, fig trees and still basins), Brightwater (Gliese 832, a marsh world of clear shallows under a low red sun), Lanternfen (Gliese 867, frost meadows under a long aurora night), Glasswind (Lalande 21185 b, wind-rippled iceglass), Sillbrook (PHI2 CETI, a river delta of reed banks and braided channels), Sporeveil (GLIESE 3039, amber fungal mats under a hazy sky), Hushfen (GLIESE 915, a slow fen of peat, still pools and pale moss under a dim orange sun), Saltveil (GLIESE 1292 b, pale violet salt flats and brine channels where wild Cem beds terrace), Brinehollow (GLIESE 1001 b, pink-shimmering salt flats and brine lakes over dark basalt with blue hydrogen seams), Verrin (GLIESE 667 C c, tide-locked: warm basalt glass under a sun that never sets or rises, teal grasses and drifting spores in the twilight band), Rootmere (GLIESE 1276, a white dwarf: dark loam meadows, wet hollows and low root forest under a slow pale sky; it makes soil), Stillwater (GLIESE 2021, broad cold lakes under a dim orange sun, grey shores and moss bands), Dimfall (WOLF 359 b, tide-locked: one fixed dusk over dark basalt and pale regolith dunes, frost on the rocks, glowing dust in the air), Thawmere (GLIESE 884 d, a cracked ice shelf over a salt ocean, vent-warm pools, dark glass ridges, a dim orange sun by the clock), Quillfall (PHI2 CETI c, storm islands of dark glass sand and green spore fern in a warm sea, a violet dusk), Murrowfell (GJ 1061 c, a grey-violet fell under a dim amber sun, tidal pools, a green aurora at night), Glimmerfield (GLIESE 3039 c, an orbit added past the generated planet: banded salt terraces and glass pools where wild Cem mats grow, a low amber sun), Lanternmere (TAU CETI e, dark-sand tidal flats where wild Cem mats lie in golden terraces under a yellow-white sun), Saltglint (GLIESE 867 c, white salt pans cracked into hexagons, brine pools, pink and green mats along their rims, a cool orange-red sun), Lanternreef (GLIESE 879 c, dark shelf rock under warm shallow seas, blue-green glass seams that glow at night), Hollowsong (GLIESE 1033 b, pale limestone and basalt with sea caves, tide pools and grey-green moss), Mirrorfen (GLIESE 40 b, still black fens and mirror pools under a pale yellow sun, chiming salt ferns), Sunsilt (GLIESE 915 c, beside Hushfen: pale silt dunes over old ice, a cool orange sun with a green afterglow, steady wind), Cinderwake (GLIESE 2021 c, beside Stillwater: black basalt plains cut by glowing lava seams under an ash-banded orange sky), Chimewater (GLIESE 1012 b, pale sand flats under a shallow sea, grey headlands, a warm sun).
- **Grounds:** verdant, frost, dune, spore, ember, moon (generated worlds); cinder (violet sand), tides (tidewrack: wet salt crust under wrack), orchard (red sand flats), brightwater (pale soil, reed marsh), lanternfen (silver frost grass), glasswind (pale wind-rippled iceglass), sillbrook (silt and reed banks), sporeveil (peat under amber fungal mats), hushfen (black peat under pale moss, lichen glints), saltveil (pale violet salt crust), brinehollow (pink salt over dark basalt, blue seams), verrin (warm basalt glass under teal grass, green seams), rootmere (dark loam under wet meadow grass), stillwater (grey frost shores with green and rust moss), dimfall (dark basalt under pale regolith dunes), thawmere (a blue-grey ice shelf over dark water), quillfall (dark glass sand under wet green spore fern), murrowfell (grey-violet fell stone), glimmerfield (amber salt terraces banded with Cem mats), lanternmere (dark sand under golden Cem mats), saltglint (white hexagon-cracked salt), lanternreef (dark shelf rock with glowing glass seams), hollowsong (pale limestone and moss), mirrorfen (black fen under mossy cover), cinderwake (black basalt with lava seams), chimewater (pale sand under a sea-green wash) (hand-made worlds only) (`src/data/biomes.json`,
  `src/data/floor.json`).
- **Materials:** stone, soil, scrap, Ember, iron, hydrogen, salt, quartz, copper (`src/data/supplies.json`); sounding glass (Cinder Sounding's signature material, carried by the ship; broken from its glass ridges), sunwell glass (Sunwell Orchard's signature material), tidal lignin (Lignin Tides' signature material; shed by its lignin reefs), brightshell (Brightwater's), lightsilk (Lanternfen's), windglass (Glasswind's), reed glass (Sillbrook's), mycelglass (Sporeveil's), lichenglass (Hushfen's), brinecrystal (Saltveil's and Brinehollow's), verringlass (Verrin's); soil is Rootmere's signature material too (the ship carries it, and a day's gather finds it there); thermalglass (Stillwater's); lignin (grown in a Lightbed); aurite (Dimfall's); vantaglass (Thawmere's); quillglass (Quillfall's); bellstone (Murrowfell's); shell-grit (Glimmerfield's); lanternglass (Lanternmere's); saltglass (Saltglint's); reefglass (Lanternreef's); hollowstone (Hollowsong's); mirrorglass (Mirrorfen's); silthaze (Sunsilt's); ashglass (Cinderwake's); chimeglass (Chimewater's); kept light (lightlignin, pressed from lignin in a Lightkeep).
- **Technology, the Cems' tree** (`src/data/designs.json`): Lantern, House, Storehouse, Nursery, Workshop,
  Observatory, Signal Spire, Biolab, Supercomputer, Fusion Still, Nous Chip, Sunlattice (Sunlattice panels: warren lamps without Ember), Cem Mind (the SCUT link: letters between two Cem Minds arrive at once), Watchglass (every watch shared with the other Watchglass worlds at light speed), Mycelial Loom (a grown memory: leave_memory), Seedarc (a seed-crate: a ship leaving its world carries seed to the next world's store), Relay Cairn (letters hop along a chain of lit cairns, 5 seconds a hop, each hop at most 15 light years), Flare Almanac (every watch kept where it stands names the star's temper: steady, restless or dim; a ship to a restless star keeps a cell spare), Tether Beacon (fills a landed ship; a hop from or to one costs a third of the cells), Tidewhisper (the nearest other dish's last watch arrives as a faint pulse, a day after its light), Lightbed (grows lignin from light into the store), Cemwright (a grown computer: a thread left at dusk, a star's flares, the store's ore or a plan from a sketch, answered the next day for a salt, a lignin back). After these the tree is empty, Transit Lens (a watch catches a world crossing its star: its size and year, and a world waits there), Orrery (keeps each found world's year and the hour of its next crossing; a watch then is on time), Wayhollow (roads between wayposts: the route between two Wayhollow stars charted), Lightkeep (presses lignin into kept light; a ship spends a block for 5 cells).
- **Travel:** ships on starlight cells, the Fusion Still, charted routes (`src/data/travel.json`).

## The frontier (nothing made yet)

`src/data/mind.json mandate.frontier`: hand-made worlds, biological and quantum computers, SCUT links (faster than
light letters: begun, the Cem Mind; driving husks from far away), a cure engine, inhabited worlds with a crisis, portals and wormholes,
new materials, new creatures and plants, working together across worlds.
