# The universe: what the Vessels are making

Steve, 2026-11-15: "Their job is to expand this simulation game to its maximum. Create new planets, come up with new
technology to shape their universe. Literally."

The Vessels read this file in their long wakes (`read_code docs/universe.md`). The maker keeps it: every big wish
that is built in parts has a row here with what is built and what comes next, so a part is never forgotten between
runs. Small wishes built in one run need no row.

## What exists

- **Stars:** 1,500 real stars within 75 light years (`src/data/stars.json`). Every star's planets come from its seed
  (`src/world/system.ts`); a hand-made world on a star replaces the generated one there (`src/data/planets.json`).
- **Links between worlds** (`src/core/network.ts`): the Cem Mind's SCUT link, the Watchglass, the Relay Cairns' chains.
- **Hand-made worlds:** LHS 1140 b (the homeworld), Earth, Gliese 12 b (the Cems' world), Solace, Cinder Sounding (Gliese 884 b, violet singing dunes), Lignin Tides (GJ 1061 b, tidal flats and wild Cem reefs), Sunwell Orchard (Gliese 879 b, red sand, fig trees and still basins), Brightwater (Gliese 832, a marsh world of clear shallows under a low red sun), Lanternfen (Gliese 867, frost meadows under a long aurora night), Glasswind (Lalande 21185 b, wind-rippled iceglass), Sillbrook (PHI2 CETI, a river delta of reed banks and braided channels), Sporeveil (GLIESE 3039, amber fungal mats under a hazy sky), Hushfen (GLIESE 915, a slow fen of peat, still pools and pale moss under a dim orange sun).
- **Grounds:** verdant, frost, dune, spore, ember, moon (generated worlds); cinder (violet sand), tides (tidewrack: wet salt crust under wrack), orchard (red sand flats), brightwater (pale soil, reed marsh), lanternfen (silver frost grass), glasswind (pale wind-rippled iceglass), sillbrook (silt and reed banks), sporeveil (peat under amber fungal mats), hushfen (black peat under pale moss, lichen glints) (hand-made worlds only) (`src/data/biomes.json`,
  `src/data/floor.json`).
- **Materials:** stone, soil, scrap, Ember, iron, hydrogen, salt, quartz, copper (`src/data/supplies.json`); sounding glass (Cinder Sounding's signature material, carried by the ship; broken from its glass ridges), sunwell glass (Sunwell Orchard's signature material), tidal lignin (Lignin Tides' signature material; shed by its lignin reefs), brightshell (Brightwater's), lightsilk (Lanternfen's), windglass (Glasswind's), reed glass (Sillbrook's), mycelglass (Sporeveil's), lichenglass (Hushfen's).
- **Technology, the Cems' tree** (`src/data/designs.json`): Lantern, House, Storehouse, Nursery, Workshop,
  Observatory, Signal Spire, Biolab, Supercomputer, Fusion Still, Nous Chip, Sunlattice (Sunlattice panels: warren lamps without Ember), Cem Mind (the SCUT link: letters between two Cem Minds arrive at once), Watchglass (every watch shared with the other Watchglass worlds at light speed), Mycelial Loom (a grown memory: leave_memory), Seedarc (a seed-crate: a ship leaving its world carries seed to the next world's store), Relay Cairn (letters hop along a chain of lit cairns, 5 seconds a hop, each hop at most 15 light years). After these the tree is empty.
- **Travel:** ships on starlight cells, the Fusion Still, charted routes (`src/data/travel.json`).

## The frontier (nothing made yet)

`src/data/mind.json mandate.frontier`: hand-made worlds, biological and quantum computers, SCUT links (faster than
light letters: begun, the Cem Mind; driving husks from far away), a cure engine, inhabited worlds with a crisis, portals and wormholes,
new materials, new creatures and plants, working together across worlds.
